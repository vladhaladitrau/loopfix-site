How the free audit works

Three steps, no integration needed

01

You export conversations

200–500 recent chats from your agent, as a CSV or JSON export, or straight from Langfuse or LangSmith.

02

We find the failures

Names, emails, phone numbers and IBANs are removed first. Then every conversation is checked against your policy, and mistakes are grouped by type.

03

You get the report

Your top failure types, how often each happens, a real example of each, and what to change to fix it.

After the audit

  • You get the result as a reviewable pull request or file, plus a before/after report.
